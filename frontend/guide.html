AQ-TLC mobile quick guide

1. Upload or take a plate image. 2. Use Lines to mark the origin and solvent front. 3. Use Marks to add sample positions, then calculate lanes. 4. Inspect profiles and use Edit to add, move, resize, or delete peaks. 5. Use Table for relative quantitation or calibration.

Peak areas are calculated from the normalized analytical profile between the dashed integration boundaries. Shaded bands are visual aids. Export a project before moving between devices or sites; browser-local recovery is origin-specific.

RGB projection settings are image-weighting controls, not physical spectrometer measurements. Wavelength bandwidth remains unavailable until it has a validated model.
