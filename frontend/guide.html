Start-to-finish workflow

Load the plate imageChoose Upload image for a gallery/file image or Take photo to use the rear camera. Images over 16 MB are rejected; larger images are resized for mobile analysis.
Prepare the imageUse Crop to remove unused background and Rotate to make the plate upright. Crop and rotate before drawing annotations.
Mark the runUse Lines to drag one line across the origin and one across the solvent front. Keep the origin below the front. Then choose Marks and tap once at each sample application position along the origin.
Calculate lanesTap the green radar button in the right-hand controls. A blue lane box is created for each spotting mark and the first lane becomes active.
Review the profileOpen Profile. Tune Polarity, Sensitivity, Distance and Width if automatic peak detection needs adjustment, then correct individual peaks where required.
Quantify and reportOpen Table, choose Rel. %, Calib. or MW, enter any standards, then return to Profile and choose PDF lane or PDF all.
Session warning: finish and export your report before refreshing, closing the tab, changing browser or moving to another device. The current Mobile app does not provide a project-file export for reopening an analysis later.

Image tab: tools and gestures

  • Pan: drag empty image space to move the plate. Pinch with two fingers to zoom and pan together; a mouse wheel also zooms.
  • Crop: drag a box around the required region and release to apply it. Cropping creates a new working image and clears existing lines, marks and lanes.
  • Rotate: drag horizontally for fine rotation. A double-tap/double-click rotates 90° clockwise.
  • Lines: drag horizontally across the plate. The lower boundary is treated as the origin and the upper boundary as the solvent front.
  • Marks: tap each sample position. A mark placed near the origin line snaps onto it.
  • Select: drag a line, mark or lane to reposition it. Tap a lane to make it the active lane shown in Profile and Table.

Top controls

  • New discards the current analysis after confirmation and returns to image selection.
  • Reset restores the original uploaded image and clears crop, rotation, lines, marks and lanes.
  • Undo restores the most recent recorded edit. Up to 50 recent snapshots are held during the session.
  • Settings contains RGB projection presets, a custom weighting label and Invert colours.
RGB controls are image-processing weightings. The wavelength labels do not turn the phone into a monochromator or spectrometer, and no wavelength-bandwidth control is exposed.

Profile tab: detection and manual editing

Automatic peaks are generated when lanes are calculated. The compact controls beside the image apply to the analysis:

  • Polarity D / S / B: choose default, dark-spot or bright-spot signal handling to match the appearance of the plate.
  • Sensitivity: controls how readily weaker peaks are detected.
  • Distance: controls the minimum separation between detected peaks.
  • Width: controls the threshold used for the automatic peak display band and the initial width of a newly added manual peak.
  • ↺: restores the three peak-detection settings to their defaults.

Edit menu

  • Add: tap an empty position on the densitogram to add a manual peak.
  • Move: drag a peak apex to reposition it, or drag either vertical integration boundary to resize the measured interval.
  • Delete: tap a peak apex to remove that peak.
  • Clear: removes every peak from the active lane.
  • Detect: runs automatic detection again with the current settings and replaces manual edits.

View menu

Choose Zoom and drag on the chart, choose Pan to move a zoomed chart, or choose Fit to return to the full profile. A mouse wheel can also zoom horizontally.

Gold — automatically detected peak.
Red with an asterisk — manually added or adjusted peak.
How area is measured: peak area is the baseline-corrected trapezoidal area of the normalized analytical profile between the two vertical integration boundaries. The shaded band is a visual aid.

Table tab: choose the result type

Rel. %

Reports each peak as a percentage of the total peak area in the active lane. AbsR is an optional response correction: corrected area is area divided by AbsR. Leave it at 1 when no correction is required.

Calib.

Set known peaks to S and enter their values; set unknowns to A. N excludes a peak from calibration. One standard gives a line through zero; two or more valid standards use linear regression across all lanes.

MW

For gels or blots, set at least two peaks with known positive molecular weights to S, then set unknowns to A. The app interpolates or extrapolates piecewise in log10(MW) versus Rf space.

Peak and lane names can be edited directly. Calibration standards are collected from every lane in the current image, not only the active lane.

Export a report

  • PDF lane prepares a report for the active lane.
  • PDF all prepares a multi-page report for every lane.
  • The report uses the currently selected Table mode. In the print window choose Print or Save as PDF. If pop-ups are blocked, the app downloads a printable HTML report instead.

For more reliable results

  • Photograph the plate square-on with even illumination and minimal glare or shadow.
  • Keep the origin and solvent-front lines parallel and place them at the true physical boundaries.
  • Use compact, well-separated sample spots and avoid overloaded or streaked lanes.
  • Check every lane box, profile, integration boundary and standard assignment before reporting.
  • Treat AQ-TLC as an analytical aid. Validate image capture, detection and calibration against the requirements of your laboratory method.
Data handling: the plate image is sent to the selected Streamlit app for analysis. Do not upload sensitive material until the deployment's access controls and your organisation's privacy requirements have been confirmed.
